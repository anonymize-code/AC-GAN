# Supplementary Materials

This folder contains supplementary materials for the paper "Advancing Stress Detection with Chaotic Attractor Informed Synthesis of PPG Signal".

## Contents

- **/figures**: Contains additional figures that could not be included in the main paper due to space limitations.
  - `attractor_comparison.jpg`: Visualization of attractor reconstructed from real signal and signal generated by AC-GAN and PPG-WGAN
  - `comparison_acc_ranking.png` and `comparison_similarity_ranking.png`: result of Nemenyi post-hoc test for a comprehensive analysis and evaluation of comparative experiment.
  - `signal_waveforms_UBFC_CLAS.png`: Waveform of real and generated signals on UBFC and CLAS datasets.
  - `tsne_analysis_UBFC_CLAS.png`: Visualization of feature distribution on UBFC and CLAS datasets.
  - `tsne_analysis_ablation.png`: Visualization of feature distribution in ablation experiments.

- **/tables**: Contains additional tables that could not be included in the main paper due to space limitations.
  - `table_similarity_UBFC_CLAS.png`: Quantitative assessment results of similarity on UBFC and CLAS datasets in comparative experiment.
  - `table_ablation_study_UBFC_CLAS.png`: Quantitative assessment results of similarity on UBFC and CLAS datasets in ablation study.
